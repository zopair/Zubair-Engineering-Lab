REGISTRATION & TRUST GUIDE

Register accurately. Apply at the official source.

Each platform has its own rules and verification. Use this checklist, then follow the current instructions on the official website.

Before creating an account

01

Accurate identity

Use your legal name and real country of residence. Never use a false identity, address or location.

02

Consistent profile

Keep your role, skills, experience and projects consistent with your actual work. Do not invent certificates or years of experience.

03

Official verification

Use your own email and phone. Complete verification on the platform itself. Do not share verification codes with other people.

04

Protect your money

Do not pay to obtain a job or share card details or passwords in unverified recruiter messages.

Common platforms

Upwork

Freelance contracts and projects. Read account, identity and profile requirements before applying.

Official signup ↗Avoid duplicate accounts and false information. Follow platform communication and payment rules.

Fiverr

A freelance services marketplace. Define your service and deliverables clearly.

Official signup ↗Describe what you can deliver accurately. Do not present other people's work as your own.

LinkedIn

A professional network for profiles, job searches and connections.

Official signup ↗Be cautious of messages demanding money or immediate off-platform contact.

Freelancer.com

A marketplace for freelance projects and bids.

Official signup ↗Check current fees, withdrawals and verification for your country on the official site.

Wellfound

Startup roles, some remote depending on location and role requirements.

Official signup ↗Check location eligibility. Remote does not necessarily mean worldwide.
